# My security policy

I treat local text handling, the WebAssembly boundary, the static server, and corpus intake as security-sensitive parts of Àmì.

## My reporting path

I have not published the GitHub repository yet, and I do not have a verified private reporting address in this checkout. I will enable GitHub private vulnerability reporting before I publish a public release. I cannot promise a confidential intake channel from this repository until I enable private reporting.

I do not put tokens, private keys, or personal writing in a public issue. I do not request credentials through chat. I never store or commit a credential. I use a secure temporary credential path for a single push and clear it immediately after that push.

## My review scope

I review input-length checks, UTF-8 and grapheme handling, WebAssembly allocation and freeing, static path resolution, content security policy, source checksums, and generated artifacts. I keep the writing path offline after the local page and engine load.

## My response

I aim to acknowledge a private report within seven days after I have a private reporting route. I work on a fix, check the affected version, and coordinate disclosure through my private reporting route. I do not promise a response time while the private route is unavailable.

I do not commit a vulnerability report, exploit sample, secret, or raw writing sample to the public repository.
