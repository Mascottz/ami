# I add one cited Igbo form family

## Why I want this

I currently have one source-checked Hausa form family, `gida` and `gidaje`. I want one equally careful Igbo example before I make broader morphology claims.

## My scope

- I choose one short, clearly documented Igbo form pair from a source with compatible reuse terms.
- I record the source URL, exact page or revision, date, license, attribution, and the word forms I use.
- I add the pair to my data table and add a focused test that keeps the suggestion optional.
- I leave the typed form unchanged until I explicitly accept a candidate.

## I call this done when

- I can trace each form to the cited source.
- I can explain the license basis in my data notice.
- I have a passing test for the exact pair.
- I describe the work as one example, not a complete Igbo morphology system.

## My boundaries

I do not add a form family without source review. I do not present one pair as a universal rule, and I do not apply prescriptive judgments to Nigerian Pidgin.
